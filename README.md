# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using retail transaction data to uncover purchasing patterns, customer segments, product performance, and subscription trends. The objective is to generate actionable business insights that help improve customer engagement, optimize marketing strategies, and support data-driven business decisions.

The dataset contains approximately **3,900 customer purchase transactions** across multiple product categories.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wanted to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve sales performance, customer satisfaction, and long-term customer retention.

The analysis aimed to answer the following business question:

> **How can customer shopping data be leveraged to identify purchasing trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

To address this, the project analyzed:

- Customer demographics
- Product category performance
- Discount effectiveness
- Customer loyalty and repeat purchases
- Subscription adoption and spending behavior

## 📊 Business Questions Answered

The project answers several business-driven questions, including:

- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- How do discounts influence customer spending?
- Which product categories perform the best?
- What percentage of customers have subscribed to the membership program?
- How does subscriber spending compare to non-subscribers?
- Which products receive the highest customer ratings?
- What are the purchasing patterns of repeat customers?
- How do customer demographics influence shopping behavior?

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- Analyzed **3,900+ retail transactions** to uncover customer shopping patterns.
- Identified a **27% subscription adoption rate** among customers.
- Compared spending behavior between subscribers and non-subscribers to understand customer value.
- Evaluated product performance, customer ratings, and repeat purchase behavior.
- Identified high-value customer segments and key revenue-generating product categories.
- Generated actionable insights to support marketing optimization and customer retention strategies.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Design targeted marketing campaigns for high-value customer segments.
- Increase subscription adoption through personalized promotions and loyalty incentives.
- Focus inventory and promotional efforts on top-performing product categories.
- Improve customer retention by rewarding repeat customers with exclusive offers.
- Optimize discount strategies based on customer purchasing behavior and product performance.
